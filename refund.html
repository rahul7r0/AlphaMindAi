# Cancellation & Refund Policy **Last Updated: 27 August 2026** This Cancellation & Refund Policy explains the rules applicable to purchases and subscriptions made through AlphaMind AI. ## 1. Subscription Cancellation Users may request cancellation of a subscription by contacting AlphaMind AI through the contact information provided on the website. Cancellation does not automatically guarantee a refund. ## 2. Refunds Refund requests will be reviewed on a case-by-case basis. Where a refund is approved, the refund will generally be processed through the original payment method used for the transaction. Processing times may depend on the payment provider or financial institution. ## 3. Duplicate Payments If a user is charged more than once for the same subscription due to a duplicate transaction, the user may contact us with the relevant transaction details for review. If the duplicate charge is confirmed, the additional payment may be refunded. ## 4. Technical Issues If a user is unable to access a paid service because of a verified technical issue on our side, the user may contact us for assistance. We may investigate the issue and, where appropriate, provide a suitable resolution. ## 5. Incorrect Purchases Users should verify the selected plan, price, and subscription duration before completing payment. Requests related to an incorrect plan or accidental purchase will be reviewed individually. ## 6. Non-Refundable Situations A refund may not be available where: * The user has already substantially used the paid service * The request is unrelated to a verified service issue * The information provided for the refund request is incomplete or inaccurate * The transaction does not match our records ## 7. Contact for Refund Requests For cancellation or refund requests, contact AlphaMind AI through the contact information provided on the website. Please include relevant transaction information so that the request can be reviewed. **AlphaMind AI**
